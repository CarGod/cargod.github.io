How it works

FanFan helps when you get stuck—and when a word returns.

01 · FanFan mode brings your library back to the page

Saved words are marked with a restrained orange highlight on any page. Click one to read the stored meaning, examples, and synonyms—no network request and no additional model cost.

02 · For a new word, understand this occurrence

Select a word, phrase, or sentence. AI first works out what it means in that specific passage, then gives a concise meaning, English definition, examples, and synonyms.

03 · Read long pages and videos without losing the thread

Translate the hovered paragraph or the whole page. On YouTube, turn on bilingual subtitles and adjust the text size, display mode, and backdrop.

04 · Save the context, then review on schedule

Each card keeps its sentence, page, and date. Four confidence levels drive spaced review; the dashboard shows what is due, your streak, and familiarity.

05 · Keep ownership of your data

Cards, settings, and keys stay in your browser by default and can be exported as JSON or CSV. Optional sync writes to a private repository in your own GitHub account.

P

Privacy by design

Know where your data goes before you install.

FanFan mode matches the current page against your local library on-device and sends nothing to a model. Only when you ask for an explanation, translation, or subtitle translation does the required content go directly to your chosen provider. There is no developer-operated relay server and no advertising, analytics, or tracking service.
